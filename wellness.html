How appointments work

Simple,
Walk-Through Visits.

01

Scheduling

Email us your preferred appointment date and time, or call our office. Our team will assist you with scheduling your visit.

info@360infusioncenter.com
(352) 549-9962

02

Brief intake

Our Nurse Practitioner will review your medical history, current medications, and treatment plan before proceeding with your infusion or injection.

03

Treatment administration

Treatments are currently administered by our Nurse Practitioner under the oversight of our Medical Director. Relax in a private suite with a recliner, TV and Wi-Fi.

04

After your visit

No downtime. 24/7 clinical pharmacist support is available for urgent, non-emergency concerns related to your treatment — call (352) 549-9962 and select the support option.
