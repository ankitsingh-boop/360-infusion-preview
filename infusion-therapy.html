How referral works

From Order to First Infusion.

Most patients are scheduled within days of our office receiving a complete order and insurance authorization.

01

Referral

Your doctor sends the order, or you request a wellness visit directly.

02

Insurance Verification

We check benefits and handle prior authorization where needed.

03

Scheduling

Prompt appointments, including late hours Tuesday and Thursday.

04

Your Infusion

Administered by our Nurse Practitioner in a private suite, with Medical Director oversight.

05

Follow-Up

We coordinate with your physician and plan your next visit.

What to expect

Your Visit, Step by Step.

01
Check-in

Arrive 15 minutes early for your first visit. Bring ID, insurance card and medication list.

02
Pre-assessment

Our Nurse Practitioner reviews your medical history, current medications and treatment plan before proceeding.

03
Your infusion

Settle into a private suite with a recliner, TV and Wi-Fi while your medication is administered.

04
Observation

Some medications require a short observation period. We will tell you in advance.

05
Follow-up

We send a visit summary to your prescribing physician and schedule your next appointment.

Bring to your first visit

  • Photo ID & insurance card
  • Current medication list
  • Completed patient forms
  • Comfortable clothing

Insurance & Affordability

We Help Take the Confusion Out of Insurance.

Insurance coverage and network participation vary by plan. Our team can assist with benefits verification — including out-of-network options when applicable — and explain your costs before treatment begins.

FAQ

Infusion Therapy Questions.

All FAQs
Biologic and specialty infusions are physician-directed, so they require an order from your prescribing provider. Wellness IV and weight management visits can be requested directly.
Treatments are currently administered by our Nurse Practitioner under the oversight of our Medical Director, who provides remote clinical consultation when needed and conducts periodic medical chart reviews. 24/7 clinical pharmacist support is available for urgent treatment-related concerns.
It depends on the medication. Some injections take about 15 minutes; many biologic infusions run one to two hours plus any observation time your medication requires. We will tell you what to expect when we schedule you.
